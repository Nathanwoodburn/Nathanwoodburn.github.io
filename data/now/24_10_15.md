# HNSDoH upgrade

I've just added regions to HNSDoH to allow users to select which nodes they would like to connect to. There are currently 5 regions: Australia, Europe, North America, Asia and Asia Pacific (both Asia and Australia nodes).

Selecting nodes closer to your physical location can make resolution faster.

Additionally most of the nodes now support IPv6 connections.

# HNS Email Service

I've update my HNS email service to now have an alternative shorter email domain. Rather than yourdomain@login.hns.au you can now also use yourdomain@e.hns.au. Both these domains will be routed to an inbox only yourdomain can access.
