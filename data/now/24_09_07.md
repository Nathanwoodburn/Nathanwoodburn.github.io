# Stables Card

I've been playing around with an Australian based crypto card company called [Stables](http://stables.money/). They have competitive fees when converting USDC to AUD to spend in person. I love how easy it is to top up. Have a look at this video showing a top up using the Noble chain [here](https://youtu.be/iK5PG0oyNU0).

# CUSTOMISATIONs

I've been on uni break this week so I've been customising my laptop.
